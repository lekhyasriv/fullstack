Anil Neerukonda Institute of Technology and Sciences

About the College

ANITS (Anil Neerukonda Institute of Technology and Sciences) is a prestigious engineering college located in Visakhapatnam.
It was established in 2001 and offers undergraduate and postgraduate programs.
The campus encourages innovation and only theoretical learning practical learning through workshops and projects.
